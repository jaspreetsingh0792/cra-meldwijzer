# 7. Other roles

The Article 14 reporting duty lies with the manufacturer. Others have a different role.

## Importers and distributors

- Selling under the **manufacturer's** name, without substantial modification: **no separate
  Article 14 reporting duty** [src:ncsc_melden]. Tell the manufacturer immediately what you know,
  and support its report.
- Selling under **your own** name or trademark, or after a **substantial modification**: you are
  treated as the manufacturer [src:cra_art21_22]. Use the full runbook.
- Tip: check your white-label and private-label contracts now. The contract's labels ("OEM",
  "reseller") do not decide this; the legal test does.

## Component suppliers

Do you sell a component separately on the market (a software library, a module, a chip with
firmware)? Then you are the manufacturer of **that component**, and you report for it. The
manufacturer of the final product reports separately for its product [src:ncsc_melden].

Downstream manufacturers will ask you for information under time pressure. Prepare: know which
customers use which versions, and have an advisory channel ready.

## Open-source software stewards

Reporting obligations for stewards apply from **11 December 2027**, and are narrower than a
manufacturer's [src:cra_art24_3] [src:ec_cra_reporting]. Until then there is no Article 14 duty
for the steward role. If you also sell the product commercially, you are a manufacturer for that
product.

Non-commercial open-source maintainers fall outside the CRA. Manufacturers that integrate their
code may contact them about vulnerabilities.

## Business customers and users

Using a product does not create a CRA reporting duty. But your organisation may have its own duties
under the **Cyberbeveiligingswet** or the **GDPR**; see [chapter 6](06-other-regimes.md). Tell the
manufacturer what you observe: your report may be the evidence of exploitation that starts their
clock.

## Researchers and everyone else

Anyone can report vulnerabilities, cyber threats, incidents and near misses **voluntarily**
[src:cra_art15]. In the Netherlands you can do this through the NCSC [src:ncsc_melden].
