# Disclaimer / Vrijwaring

## English

This open-source runbook is an informational aid for incident preparation and assessment. It is
not legal advice, does not determine your legal obligations, and does not submit reports on your
behalf.

The Cyber Resilience Act and related laws may apply differently depending on the product, the
manufacturer, the incident facts and the organisation. This project may contain errors or become
outdated, including when official guidance, reporting interfaces or legal interpretations change.

Verify reportability, deadlines and reporting routes against the applicable legislation and current
official guidance. Where an event may trigger a statutory deadline, escalate promptly to your
incident-response and legal contacts and, where appropriate, the designated CSIRT. Do not delay
time-sensitive action because this tool or its sources are incomplete.

The project does not guarantee legal compliance, successful submission or acceptance of a report.
Do not enter credentials, private keys or unnecessary personal or confidential information. Case
data and exported files may contain sensitive information; handle them according to your
organisation's incident-response and information-handling policies.

the RDI.
