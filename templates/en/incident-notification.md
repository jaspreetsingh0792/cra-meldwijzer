# Notification: severe incident

> Template from CRA Meldwijzer. Not legal advice; not legally reviewed. Check the current NCSC form before submitting.

| | |
|---|---|
| NCSC form, "Welk soort melding maak je?" | Melding - over een ernstig incident |
| Deadline | Without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware (not of the early warning). Information already given need not be repeated. |
| Sources | [Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (CRA), Art. 14(3)-(5) (severe incidents; severity test in 14(5))](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj); [NCSC - CRA-meldformulier](https://www.ncsc.nl/cra-meldformulier) |

## Report content

**Title** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Titel van de waarschuwing"  
_As short as possible, for example: "Actively exploited vulnerability in HR-200 router firmware". The early warning has no description field, so the title carries the message._

[fill in]

**Affected products and versions** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Welk(e) product(en) betreft het?"  
_Every affected product and version. Include older versions still in use._

[fill in]

**Manufacturer or open-source steward** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wie is de producent of open-source steward van het product?"  
_The legal entity that is the manufacturer (or steward) of the product._

[fill in]

**Discovered at (form asks "when was the vulnerability discovered")** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wanneer is de kwetsbaarheid ontdekt?"  
_On the incident notification the form's question is about the vulnerability, but its help text asks for the date and time the incident was discovered, in CET. Fill in the discovery time; mention it if no vulnerability is involved._

[fill in]

**Nature of the incident** · Required by Article 14  
NCSC form field: "Beschrijving van het incident"  
_What happened, which systems were affected, how it affects the product's security. Goes into the form's description field._

[fill in]

**Initial assessment** · Required by Article 14  
NCSC form field: "Beschrijving van het incident"  
_Severity, scope and likely impact as far as known, and how certain you are. Required by Article 14; the form has no separate field, so add it to the incident description._

[fill in]

**Incident detected at** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wanneer is het incident ontdekt?"  
_Date and time, CET (Amsterdam time)._

[fill in]

**Incident occurred at** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wanneer heeft het incident plaatsgevonden?"  
_Date and time, CET (Amsterdam time). Best estimate if not exactly known._

[fill in]

**Member States where the product is available** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "In welke EU-lidstaten is het product beschikbaar?"  
_Tick every EU Member State where you know the product has been made available. The form requires at least one._

[fill in]

**Suspected unlawful or malicious acts?** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wordt het incident vermoedelijk door een onrechtmatige of kwaadwillende handeling veroorzaakt?"  
_Yes or no on the form. Keep a short explanation in your case file._

[fill in]

**Status of the incident** · Required by the NCSC form  
NCSC form field: "Wat is de status van het incident?"  
_The form offers: cause unclear, under investigation / cause clear, not resolved / cause clear, resolved._

[fill in]

**Corrective or mitigating measures taken** · Required by Article 14  
NCSC form field: "Welke maatregelen zijn getroffen om herhaling in de toekomst te voorkomen?"  
_The form asks about preventing recurrence. Article 14 asks for the corrective or mitigating measures already taken: describe both here._

[fill in]

**Measures users can take** · Required by Article 14  
NCSC form field: "Welke maatregelen zijn er voor eindgebruikers beschikbaar?"  
_What users should do now: update, workaround, configuration change, disconnecting._

[fill in]

**Sensitivity of the information** · Required where applicable  
_Whether details could help attackers (for example before a fix exists), and why. Article 14 asks for this where applicable; the form has no separate field, so state it in the description and check whether MijnNCSC lets you mark the report as sensitive._

[fill in]
